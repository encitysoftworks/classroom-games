Keys

  • Space → Enter Page Down or a click: next step, then next word
  • ← Page Up: back
  • 1–4: jump to word / picture / sentence / Japanese
  • Grammar slides: Space also swaps in new words. W or a click on the word swaps it any time
  • S: say the word (and the sentence, once it is showing)
  • J: show or hide the Japanese right away
  • E: edit this word (sentence, Japanese, picture)
  • B or .: blank screen (most clickers have this button)
  • F: full screen   Esc: back to the word list
  • What's missing? Space goes look → close your eyes → guess → answer. W words on/off.
  • Fast flash: Space pause, ← → step, W word on/after/off.
