Who is the Imposter?

Everyone in your group has the same "best memory" card. But one person is the Imposter, and one part of their card is a secret. Talk, listen, then point!

グループ全員が同じ「いちばんの思い出」カードを持っています。でも1人はインポスター。カードの1か所がひみつです。話して、聞いて、指さそう！

3 to 5 players, each on a Chromebook.
